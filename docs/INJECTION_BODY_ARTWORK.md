# Injection body artwork

`public/injection-body.webp` is a transparent 1254 × 1254 front/rear mannequin asset generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then encoded as WebP (207,578 bytes). Each half is rendered separately; site overlays remain live UI elements. Front labels use mirror orientation, with the member's left on screen left. No account photos or private health records were used to generate it.

## Generation prompt

Create a polished realistic 3D clinical mannequin turnaround: exactly TWO matching full-body adult male anatomical mannequins, FRONT view on left, REAR view on right. Smooth neutral slate-blue/lavender material with soft rim light, recognisable natural chest, abdomen, shoulders, arms, buttocks and thighs, moderate natural muscle definition. Nonsexual medical mannequin: no genital detail, no facial detail, no hair. Arms slightly away from torso, hands at upper thighs, legs separated slightly. Orthographic straight-on, identical scale and pose. Square canvas, two equal vertical halves; center front figure at 25% canvas width, rear at 75%. In each half, body spans 12% to 88% of that half width. Top of head at 3% canvas height, chin 14%, shoulder tops 20%, waist 41%, pelvis 50%, buttocks center 51%, upper thighs 60%, knees 74%, feet 97%. Feet fully visible. True transparent background everywhere outside the mannequins, including between arms and torso and legs. Even illumination readable on both light lavender and navy UI backgrounds. No text, labels, numbers, codes, arrows, coloured regions, dots, injection markers, UI panels, or watermark. Site status regions will be rendered separately by the app, never paint them into this asset.
